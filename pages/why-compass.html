Why Compass?

Compass Consult is not simply providing additional resource. We are providing a specialist employer engagement and system-development function that strengthens the skills ecosystem across the UK.

What Compass Consult Brings

  • Extensive sector experience

    Deep knowledge of the employability and skills sector, built over 20 years working with partners, providers, employers and stakeholders.

  • Employer engagement expertise

    Proven track record in building strategic employer relationships, generating opportunities and brokering real pathways for young people.

  • Understanding of local authority environments

    Experience of working within local government and partnership structures, navigating priorities and governance.

  • Knowledge of policy

    Up-to-date understanding of national and local employment and skills policy, funding landscapes and programme requirements.

  • Multi-stakeholder collaboration

    Skilled in bringing together employers, education providers, boroughs, commissioners and delivery partners to achieve shared outcomes.

  • Independent perspective

    Operating across sectors and places, providing a neutral, impartial view that helps identify gaps, reduce duplication and drive collaboration.

  • System development focus

    Beyond activity delivery, we strengthen the skills system by improving processes, data use, coordination and continuous improvement.

  • Flexible and scalable capacity

    Our consultancy model allows us to increase or decrease resource as needs change, while maintaining continuity and high-quality delivery.

Compass Consult acts as a trusted extension of your team, helping to connect employers, unlock opportunities and create lasting impact for young people and communities.

Specialist expertise. Independent insight. Stronger system outcomes.

Delivering value for employers, partners and communities

  • Builds stronger employer relationships
  • Generates jobs, training and opportunities
  • Reduces duplication and improves coordination
  • Provides intelligence to shape provision and policy
  • Drives better outcomes for people and communities
